The Borella Story

A Welcoming Space for Coffee Lovers & Sweet Tooth Cravers

Conveniently nestled at 89 Dr N. M. Perera Mawatha (Cotta Road), Cakes and Coffee was born out of a simple passion: to serve authentic, aromatic coffee alongside melt-in-the-mouth, freshly baked cakes.

Whether you need an espresso boost at 9:00 AM on your morning commute, a quiet corner to brainstorm over an iced caramel latte, or a surprise chocolate fudge gateau for an evening celebration, we prepare everything with care and premium ingredients.
